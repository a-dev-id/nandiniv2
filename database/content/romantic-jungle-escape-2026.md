## Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens: Private Rainforest Villas and Royal Suites for Couples in Payangan, Greater Ubud

![Garden View Royal Suite](https://nandinibali.com/storage/images/gallery/villa.jpg)

While much of Bali's romance marketing centres on beach clubs and clifftop sunsets, many couples planning an anniversary or honeymoon in 2026 are looking inland — toward the rainforest valleys of Greater Ubud, where privacy comes from the landscape itself rather than a "do not disturb" sign. The hillsides above the Ayung River, in the rural district of Payangan, offer exactly that kind of seclusion: terraced jungle, river mist, and villas tucked into the greenery.

This guide covers one property built around that experience. Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens is a luxury jungle resort located in Banjar Susut, Desa Buahan, Payangan, within the greater Ubud area of Bali, set on a tropical hillside overlooking the Ayung River valley (Source: [Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens](https://nandinibali.com/)). Below is what it offers couples planning a romantic stay, how its villas and wellness experiences work, and how to plan the practical side of a trip there.

**Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens — Private Jungle Villas and Royal Suites Above the Ayung River**

- **Description:** Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens is a private rainforest retreat with Balinese character, built into a steep hillside above the Ayung River. Accommodation comprises private jungle villas and Royal Suites with terraces, balconies, gardens and rainforest views; each villa category has a private balcony and outdoor bathtub & rain shower, and the Panoramic Corner Jacuzzi Royal Suite features a private Jacuzzi (Source: [Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens](https://nandinibali.com/)).
- **Key differentiators for couples:** A signature Holy River experience connecting guests to the sacred Ayung River through a Balinese blessing, an on-site spa with couples treatments, riverside wellness experiences, riverfront picnics, honeymoon experiences, and a funicular that carries guests between the resort's hillside levels (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens).
- **Dining built for two:** Two restaurants — The Station Restaurant and Wild Ginger Restaurant — plus private dining, breakfast in the room, a poolside bar, afternoon tea and themed dinner nights (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens).
- **Best for:** Couples celebrating anniversaries and milestones, honeymooners, wellness-focused travelers, and anyone who wants upscale accommodation inside a genuine rainforest environment rather than an urban hotel setting.
- **Setting:** A rural hillside in Payangan, surrounded by rainforest and away from the density of central Ubud — Tegallalang Rice Terrace is about 13 km away ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Nandini+Jungle+by+Hanging+Gardens&origin_place_id=ChIJF-8ITUk90i0RAAr-P1uXV-4&destination=Tegallalang+Rice+Terrace&destination_place_id=ChIJ4wD5Iwsi0i0R7QRsOGmJGo0&travelmode=driving)) and Ubud Palace about 15 km away ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Nandini+Jungle+by+Hanging+Gardens&origin_place_id=ChIJF-8ITUk90i0RAAr-P1uXV-4&destination=Ubud+Palace&destination_place_id=ChIJS7J-82o90i0RMj8cKJr2zUs&travelmode=driving)).

### Villas and Royal Suites Built for Privacy, Not Proximity

The accommodation at Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens consists entirely of private jungle villas and Royal Suites, each with a terrace or balcony facing rainforest or the Ayung River valley (Source: [Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens](https://nandinibali.com/)). The Panoramic Corner Jacuzzi Royal Suite includes a private Jacuzzi, and gardens and panoramic jungle views give couples a self-contained romantic base where the morning view is canopy and river mist rather than a corridor.

For a tenth anniversary or honeymoon, this layout matters more than square footage. The low-density, terrain-following design means villas are distributed across the hillside rather than stacked side by side, reinforcing the retreat's secluded, intimate identity (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens). Rooms come with air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, mosquito nets, an in-room safe, tea and coffee facilities, daily housekeeping and a daily chocolate — small comforts that keep the "jungle" part of the experience scenic rather than rustic (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens).

Royal Suites are the more spacious option, and the resort explicitly supports family accommodation, so couples travelling with children for a milestone celebration are not excluded — there is a children's pool and babysitting service on site (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens). The property does not publish nightly rates in a fixed public tariff; check current villa and suite rates directly on the [official website](https://nandinibali.com/).

### The Holy River Blessing and Couples Wellness on the Ayung

The resort's signature romantic experience is the Holy River offering: a Balinese blessing ceremony that connects guests with the sacred Ayung River flowing below the property (Source: [Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens](https://nandinibali.com/)). For couples marking an anniversary, this is the kind of experience that anchors a trip — a culturally rooted ritual conducted in the river landscape itself, rather than a generic resort add-on.

Wellness programming extends well beyond the blessing. The on-site spa offers massages, body treatments, facials, beauty treatments and wellness rituals, with dedicated couples treatments and spa packages available; booking is required (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens). Riverside wellness experiences take treatments down toward the water, and the spa includes a lounge and foot bath for the slower moments between sessions.

Yoga classes and a gym round out the active side, and the broader Ubud region reinforces the wellness theme: Pura Tirta Empul, the famous Hindu water temple at Tampaksiring known for its purification pools, is about 19 km away ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Nandini+Jungle+by+Hanging+Gardens&origin_place_id=ChIJF-8ITUk90i0RAAr-P1uXV-4&destination=Pura+Tirta+Empul&destination_place_id=ChIJtTFh4PQY0i0RwewUdwFaolM&travelmode=driving)) — a natural pairing with the resort's own river blessing for couples drawn to Bali's spiritual traditions.

### Dining for Two: Restaurants, Riverfront Picnics and Private Tables

Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens runs two restaurants — The Station Restaurant and Wild Ginger Restaurant — serving Indonesian cuisine alongside broader resort dining (Source: [Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens](https://nandinibali.com/)). For couples, the more memorable options sit around the edges of the main restaurants: private dining, riverfront picnic experiences, barbecue facilities and picnic dining let the resort stage meals in secluded corners of the hillside and riverbank.

Slower romantic rhythms are well supported too. Breakfast in the room is available, as are room service, afternoon tea, a coffee shop, a snack bar and a poolside bar with a happy hour (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens). Evenings bring entertainment in the form of live music and performances, themed dinner nights and Balinese dance performances — meaning an anniversary dinner can be followed by traditional dance without a night-time drive back from Ubud.

Because the resort is rural, this on-property dining depth is a practical advantage, not just a luxury. Central Ubud's restaurant scene around Ubud Palace sits about 15 km away ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Nandini+Jungle+by+Hanging+Gardens&origin_place_id=ChIJF-8ITUk90i0RAAr-P1uXV-4&destination=Ubud+Palace&destination_place_id=ChIJS7J-82o90i0RMj8cKJr2zUs&travelmode=driving)), so most couples will dine at the resort on most nights — and the property is clearly built for that.

### Pools, the Funicular and the Hillside Landscape

The resort's infinity pool looks directly over the jungle, with a hot tub/Jacuzzi, sun loungers and pool towels provided (Source: [Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens](https://nandinibali.com/)). Tropical gardens, outdoor terraces, a picnic area and open-air seating are threaded through the grounds, with jungle and Ayung River views framing nearly every level.

One of the property's most distinctive features is its funicular — a hillside elevator connecting the resort's multiple levels, supplemented by stairways (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens). The steep terrain is what creates the sense of discovery and the river access, but it also means the resort is not wheelchair accessible and has no accessible rooms; couples who need a flat, step-free layout should look at alternatives elsewhere in the Ubud area (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens).

For couples who want to explore the landscape beyond the grounds, the resort offers hiking, walking tours, bicycle tours and bicycle rental, plus cooking classes and local culture tours for rainy afternoons (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens). Sunrise ambitions are achievable too: Mount Batur, Bali's classic sunrise trekking volcano, is about 34 km away ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Nandini+Jungle+by+Hanging+Gardens&origin_place_id=ChIJF-8ITUk90i0RAAr-P1uXV-4&destination=Mount+Batur&destination_place_id=ChIJP-7oyAP00S0RtHCGoa9FgNM&travelmode=driving)).

### Day Trips From Payangan: Rice Terraces, Ridge Walks and Waterfalls

Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens is deliberately removed from central Ubud, but the region's best-known romantic excursions remain within comfortable driving range. Tegallalang Rice Terrace — the most photographed rice-paddy landscape in the Ubud area — is about 13 km away ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Nandini+Jungle+by+Hanging+Gardens&origin_place_id=ChIJF-8ITUk90i0RAAr-P1uXV-4&destination=Tegallalang+Rice+Terrace&destination_place_id=ChIJ4wD5Iwsi0i0R7QRsOGmJGo0&travelmode=driving)), making it the easiest golden-hour outing from the resort.

The Campuhan Ridge Walk, a grassy ridgeline trail popular with couples at sunrise and late afternoon, is about 15 km away ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Nandini+Jungle+by+Hanging+Gardens&origin_place_id=ChIJF-8ITUk90i0RAAr-P1uXV-4&destination=Campuhan+Ridge+Walk&destination_place_id=ChIJ9XP0yms90i0Rn8QbqnT9E3w&travelmode=driving)). In central Ubud itself, Ubud Palace is about 15 km away, and the Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary — Ubud's famous nature preserve — is about 17 km away ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Nandini+Jungle+by+Hanging+Gardens&origin_place_id=ChIJF-8ITUk90i0RAAr-P1uXV-4&destination=Sacred+Monkey+Forest+Sanctuary&destination_place_id=ChIJZ5sY9kM90i0RVsPvSxZD7LY&travelmode=driving)), so a combined palace-and-monkey-forest day is straightforward.

Further afield, Tegenungan Waterfall sits about 25 km away ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Nandini+Jungle+by+Hanging+Gardens&origin_place_id=ChIJF-8ITUk90i0RAAr-P1uXV-4&destination=Tegenungan+Waterfall&destination_place_id=ChIJYQy97hsW0i0RXgxBuN15rsE&travelmode=driving)), and Marine Safari Bali, a family-oriented aquarium attraction, is about 31 km away ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Nandini+Jungle+by+Hanging+Gardens&origin_place_id=ChIJF-8ITUk90i0RAAr-P1uXV-4&destination=Marine+Safari+Bali&destination_place_id=ChIJr7iCed4V0i0RQaYZ3SfXfic&travelmode=driving)) for couples travelling with children. The resort's concierge, tour and ticket assistance, car rental and shuttle service handle the logistics of all of these (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens).

### Planning an Anniversary or Honeymoon Stay: Transfers, Timing and Who It Suits

The practical side of a stay is well covered for a rural property. Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens offers airport transfers, a resort shuttle, car rental, valet parking and included on-site parking, alongside a 24-hour front desk, concierge, multilingual staff, luggage storage, laundry, currency exchange and express check-in and check-out (Source: [Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens](https://nandinibali.com/)). Arranging the airport transfer in advance is the simplest way to arrive, since the property's rural Payangan address is not somewhere ride-hailing from the airport works reliably without planning.

For the celebration itself, the resort offers dedicated honeymoon experiences, wedding ceremonies and wedding services, plus gift vouchers for couples gifting a stay (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens). Private dining and private picnics can be arranged as events, which is the natural route for a surprise anniversary dinner; corporate meetings and retreats are not offered, which keeps the property's event focus on celebrations rather than conferences (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens).

Two honest caveats help set expectations. First, this is an inland river-valley resort — couples who want beachfront access or a walkable town base should choose a different part of Bali, because Payangan prioritises quiet, views and privacy over walkable shops and nightlife (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens). Second, the hillside layout involves multiple levels, stairs and the funicular, so guests who need step-free access are explicitly not well served here. Pets are not permitted, and the property is non-smoking with a designated smoking area (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens).

## Conclusion: A Rainforest Retreat for Couples Who Want Bali's Quiet Side

For couples planning a romantic Bali trip in 2026 — whether a honeymoon, a tenth anniversary or simply a milestone escape — Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens offers a clearly defined proposition: private jungle villas and Royal Suites above the Ayung River, a signature Holy River blessing, couples spa treatments, riverfront picnics and an infinity pool with jungle views, all in a secluded Payangan setting about 13–17 km from Ubud's headline attractions (Source: [Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens](https://nandinibali.com/)). It suits couples who measure romance in privacy, landscape and ritual rather than nightlife or beach access.

The trade-offs are the flip side of the same qualities: a rural location that requires transfers, a steep hillside layout, and no beach. Couples comfortable with those conditions will find a retreat purpose-built for celebration — and should check current villa and suite availability directly on the [official website](https://nandinibali.com/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens a good choice for an anniversary trip to Bali?**
It is designed for exactly that. The resort explicitly serves couples celebrating milestones, with private villas, jungle views, couples spa treatments, secluded private dining, riverfront picnics and honeymoon experiences, plus the signature Holy River Balinese blessing on the Ayung River (Source: [Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens](https://nandinibali.com/)).

**Do the villas have private pools or Jacuzzis?**
Villas feature a private balcony and outdoor bathtub & rain shower, the Panoramic Corner Jacuzzi Royal Suite features a private Jacuzzi, and all accommodation includes a terrace or balcony with rainforest views (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens). The resort also has an infinity pool with jungle views, a children's pool and a shared hot tub. Confirm which specific villa categories include a private Jacuzzi on the [official website](https://nandinibali.com/) before booking.

**How far is the resort from central Ubud?**
Ubud Palace is about 15 km away ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Nandini+Jungle+by+Hanging+Gardens&origin_place_id=ChIJF-8ITUk90i0RAAr-P1uXV-4&destination=Ubud+Palace&destination_place_id=ChIJS7J-82o90i0RMj8cKJr2zUs&travelmode=driving)) and the Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary about 17 km away ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Nandini+Jungle+by+Hanging+Gardens&origin_place_id=ChIJF-8ITUk90i0RAAr-P1uXV-4&destination=Sacred+Monkey+Forest+Sanctuary&destination_place_id=ChIJZ5sY9kM90i0RVsPvSxZD7LY&travelmode=driving)). The resort offers a shuttle service, car rental, concierge and tour assistance to make those trips easy (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens).

**Is the property suitable for guests with mobility limitations?**
No. The resort occupies a steep hillside with multiple levels, stairs and a funicular; it is not wheelchair accessible and has no accessible rooms (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens). Couples who need step-free access should consider other hotels in the Ubud area.

**Can couples hold a wedding or vow renewal at the resort?**
Yes. Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens offers wedding ceremonies, wedding services, private dining and private picnics as event types (Source: Nandini Jungle by Hanging Gardens). Contact the resort directly to confirm arrangements, availability and pricing for your dates.
